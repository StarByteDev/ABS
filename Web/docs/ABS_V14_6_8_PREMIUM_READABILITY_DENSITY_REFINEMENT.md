# ABS V14.6.8 — Premium Readability + Density Refinement

**Date:** 21 August 2026  
**Baseline:** ABS V14.6.7 Premium Professional Public Experience Build

This build is a focused visual refinement of the approved premium homepage direction. No application workflow, data source, membership logic, Pulse feature, Admin feature or MySQL/Laragon behavior is removed.

## Homepage refinements

- Increased desktop typography throughout the full homepage so navigation, hero copy, market board, intelligence cards, headlines, feature cards, market overview, plans, CTA and footer remain comfortably readable on normal desktop displays.
- Preserved the approved deep-navy, gold and cyan brand treatment and the same section order/content hierarchy.
- Reduced dead space by tightening hero/section padding, card minimum heights and lower-page spacing while allowing text to occupy the cards naturally.
- Enlarged the BTC/USDT price hierarchy, market metadata, side-card labels, sentiment legend, BTC dominance and liquidations presentation.
- Kept Market Pulse and Market Sentiment visually and logically separate. Market Sentiment remains centered in the donut without a percent sign below the value.
- Enlarged the five-asset ticker strip, trust strip, verified-headline cards and platform-advantage content.
- Improved Clarity Before Action, Market Overview KPIs/charts and Pulse plan readability without making the page oversized.
- Increased footer and shared public-page text sizes so the premium styling remains usable instead of decorative-only.
- Retuned desktop/tablet/mobile breakpoints to avoid oversized empty areas when the layout collapses.

## Shared public pages

- Increased public page hero, panel, content-card, headline and legal-document readability while retaining the existing premium visual language.
- No signed-in Pulse workspace redesign was introduced in this pass.

# ABS V14.0 Focused Product Scope

ABS V14.0 deliberately avoids presenting Alpha Block Solutions as a catalogue of unrelated products. The active market-ready website is organized around:

- **Pulse Trading Intelligence** — the flagship trading intelligence, strategy, risk and controlled execution platform.
- **Private Member Portal** — an invitation-only account reporting service.
- **Live Markets** and **Market News** — supporting daily-use information that brings users back to Pulse.

Generic blockchain-development, token, NFT, research, learning and community products are not active public product lines in this build.

Pulse itself is plan-driven. Administrators can create any customer plan and choose exactly which Pulse modules, strategies, limits and execution capabilities are available. A clean installation provides Pulse Trial as an onboarding entitlement plus two public customer memberships, Pulse Intelligence and Pulse Professional. Admin can change visibility, pricing, limits and entitlements at any time.

# ABS V14.6.10 — Premium Wide Layout + Readability Fix

## Approved visual direction

This revision keeps the final V14.6.9 homepage structure and corrects the browser-rendered proportions reported after deployment/review.

### Changes

- Desktop container widened to use the screen more effectively and remove excessive left/right empty space.
- Hero body copy and all supporting paragraph text enlarged for comfortable reading at normal zoom.
- Verified headline titles and summaries enlarged.
- Trust cards, advantages, industries, daily market intelligence, movers, plans, CTA and footer typography enlarged.
- BTC market metadata, Market Pulse, Market Sentiment, Long/Short Liquidations, Futures Open Interest/Funding and ticker strip typography refined.
- Existing compact card heights and section order retained so the page stays dense and premium rather than oversized.
- Tablet and mobile layout behavior preserved.

### Data/functionality preserved

No market logic was changed in this revision. Binance prices/chart/futures, Xoomar aggregated long/short liquidations, CoinGecko global/category data, Alternative.me Fear & Greed, live/CMS headlines, plans, Admin, MySQL/Laragon, authentication, membership and Pulse workspace remain as in V14.6.9.
